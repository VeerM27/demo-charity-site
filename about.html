About the pantry

Started in a church hall in 2014 with two tables and a borrowed van. Today we run from our own warehouse on Mill Lane.

What we believe

  • Food is a right, not a reward.
  • Nobody has to prove they are hungry.
  • Waste is a problem we can fix together.
  • Every visitor is a neighbour, not a case number.
